How Abeg works

A quick tour of the food-ordering assistant — no tech knowledge needed.

1
🗣️

You ask

Type your order, or hold the mic and just say it out loud — like talking to a waiter.

›
2
🤖

The assistant understands

A smart assistant works out what you want — and if it's unsure, it asks instead of guessing.

›
3
📦

It checks real stock

It never makes up prices or what's available — it checks the real kitchen list every time.

›
4
🧾

Your order is placed

You confirm, the order is saved, and you get a reference number. Done!

Two cloud helpers do the heavy lifting behind the assistant: 🧠 an AI “brain” for the conversation 👂 an AI “ear” that turns your voice into words

🎛️

A peek behind the counter

There's a hidden “Workshop” panel you can slide open to watch, live, everything the assistant is doing — what it looked up, what it reserved, each order as it happens — and even turn the AI's knobs yourself.

🍽️

The big moment: the last plate

What if two people order the last one at the exact same time? We show it both ways — the safe way (only one wins) and the careless way (both win and stock drops below zero). That contrast is the whole point of the demo.

For the curious, under the hood: a Python web app, a small database that holds the menu and orders, and two AI services (one for chat, one for voice).
